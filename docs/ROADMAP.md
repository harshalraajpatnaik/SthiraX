# SthiraX Technology & Industrial Scale-up Roadmap

The SthiraX scale-up roadmap outlines an engineering and commercial progression across four distinct phases:

```
[Phase 1: Lab & Pilot]       --> [Phase 2: Commercial Retrofit] --> [Phase 3: Multi-Plant Hub]   --> [Phase 4: CCTS Registry]
(Hosur 1,000t/mo Prototype)     (Hosur Commercial Operations)      (5 Plants in South India)        (National Verification)
```

---

## Phase 1: Prototype Validation & Pilot Enclosure (Q1 2025 – Q4 2025)
* **Objective**: Demonstrate accelerated CO₂ carbonation in non-structural precast elements under Indian climate conditions at the Hosur pilot facility.
* **Core Deliverables**:
  * Construction of 120 m³ gas-tight insulated curing chamber.
  * Integration of Emerson Coriolis mass-flow sensor for real-time gas dosing.
  * Formulations achieving $\ge 35\text{ MPa}$ compressive strength with 15% OPC reduction.
  * Initial NABL third-party mechanical verification break reports (IS 15658:2006).
* **Capital Requirement**: ₹ 45 – ₹ 60 Lakhs INR.
* **Status**: **COMPLETE / OPERATIONAL IN DEMO ENVIRONMENT**.

---

## Phase 2: First Commercial Retrofit & SthiraX Trace™ (Q1 2026 – Q3 2026)
* **Objective**: Transition Hosur facility to commercial continuous batch operations and deploy the SthiraX Trace™ digital product passport.
* **Core Deliverables**:
  * Continuous procurement of cryogenic liquid CO₂ from Deccan Distilleries (100 t/month).
  * First commercial supply of 10,000 sq.m interlocking pavers to Bengaluru infrastructure projects.
  * Launch of public verification portal with tamper-evident QR codes.
  * Complete lifecycle gate-to-gate inventory verification per ISO 14064-2.
* **Capital Requirement**: ₹ 85 – ₹ 110 Lakhs INR.
* **Status**: **CURRENT ACTIVE MILESTONE**.

---

## Phase 3: Regional Multi-Plant Hub Expansion (Q4 2026 – Q4 2027)
* **Objective**: Scale the asset-light retrofit model to 5 independent precast facilities across Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, and Andhra Pradesh (Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad).
* **Core Deliverables**:
  * Standardized modular retrofit hardware kit ($< ₹ 35\text{ Lakhs}$ per plant, 14-day installation downtime).
  * 120,000 tonnes aggregate annual mineralized precast capacity.
  * Regional CO₂ bulk logistics optimization reducing cryogenic transport costs by 22%.
  * Enterprise procurement master services agreements (MSAs) with Tier-1 infrastructure contractors.
* **Capital Requirement**: ₹ 3.5 – ₹ 4.5 Crores INR.
* **Status**: **PLANNED**.

---

## Phase 4: National CCTS Verification & Heavy Precast Expansion (2028+)
* **Objective**: Expand product scope to semi-structural precast elements (drainage culverts, retaining wall panels) and participate in India's compliance Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS).
* **Core Deliverables**:
  * Technical validation of reinforced precast carbonation without carbonation-induced steel corrosion.
  * Accreditation under BEE Carbon Credit Trading Scheme registry.
  * Multi-region network exceeding 25 partner facilities and 500,000 tonnes/year.
* **Capital Requirement**: ₹ 12 – ₹ 16 Crores INR.
* **Status**: **FUTURE HORIZON**.
