# Indian Standards, Regulatory Frameworks & Compliance Sources

## 1. Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) Construction Specifications

All precast concrete products manufactured through SthiraX partner facilities must conform to applicable BIS national codes:

### 1.1 IS 15658:2006 — Precast Concrete Blocks for Paving
* **Scope**: Specifications for pavers used in light, medium, and heavy-duty pedestrian and vehicular pavements.
* **Compressive Strength Grades**:
  * M-30 (Light traffic / pedestrian): $\ge 30.0\text{ MPa}$
  * M-35 (City streets / commercial walkways): $\ge 35.0\text{ MPa}$
  * M-40 / M-50 (Heavy industrial terminals / bus bays): $\ge 40.0 - 50.0\text{ MPa}$
* **Key Durability Criteria**:
  * Water absorption: Maximum $6.0\%$ by mass (individual paver $\le 7.0\%$).
  * Abrasion resistance: Maximum wear depth $\le 2.0\text{ mm}$ (tested per Annex E).
  * Minimum thickness: $60\text{ mm}$ for non-traffic, $80\text{ mm}$ for city roads, $100\text{ mm}$ for ports/terminals.

### 1.2 IS 2185 (Part 1):2005 — Concrete Masonry Units (Hollow and Solid Concrete Blocks)
* **Scope**: Solid and hollow precast load-bearing and non-load-bearing masonry units.
* **Compressive Strength**: Minimum $4.0 - 5.0\text{ MPa}$ for Grade C non-loadbearing; $\ge 10.0\text{ MPa}$ for load-bearing.
* **Block Density & Water Absorption**: Water absorption $\le 10\%$ by mass.

### 1.3 IS 1077:1992 — Common Burnt Clay Building Bricks
* Benchmark for solid precast masonry blocks replacing conventional red clay bricks.
* Compressive strength grades: 7.5, 10.0, 15.0 MPa.

### 1.4 IS 3812 (Part 1 & 2):2013 — Pulverized Fuel Ash
* Quality standards for pozzolanic fly ash utilized as a supplementary cementitious material (SCM) in SthiraX low-carbon binder formulations.

## 2. Indian Environmental & Industrial Mandates

### 2.1 MoEFCC Fly Ash Notification (December 2021)
* Mandatory utilization of 100% fly ash in all construction projects located within a 300 km radius of thermal power plants.
* Requires precast concrete manufacturers to blend minimum specified percentages of pozzolanic materials, providing regulatory alignment for SthiraX binder substitution.

### 2.2 Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS) — Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE)
* Governed under the Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act 2022.
* Establishes the domestic Indian compliance and voluntary carbon market architecture.
* SthiraX monitors draft sectoral verification methodologies for industrial emission reductions in hard-to-abate materials.

### 2.3 PESO (Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organization)
* Static and Mobile Pressure Vessels (Unfired) Rules (SMPV Rules 2016).
* Governs licensing and safety compliance for cryogenic bulk liquid $CO_2$ storage tanks and tanker decanting manifolds at partner precast facilities.
